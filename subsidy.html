Frequently Asked Questions

How long does it take for the PM Surya Ghar subsidy to be credited?

After physical installation and DISCOM net-meter inspection, the commissioning certificate is uploaded to the National Portal. The DBT subsidy is credited directly to your bank account typically within 30 days of portal approval.

What documents are required for applying?

You only need your latest electricity bill, property tax receipt or roof ownership proof, Aadhaar card, and a cancelled bank cheque linked to your Aadhaar for direct DBT transfer. Daypower handles all online uploads and submissions.

Can factories and commercial establishments claim the ₹138,000 subsidy?

The PM Surya Ghar cash subsidy is specifically for residential consumers. Commercial and Industrial consumers instead benefit from 80% Accelerated Tax Depreciation (Section 32 of Income Tax Act), Virtual Net Metering, and high bill offsets which offer significantly greater financial returns.
