About Daypower LLP

Leading the clean energy revolution with high-efficiency solar EPC engineering, experienced leadership, and end-to-end turnkey delivery across India.

☀ Our Mission & Vision

Powering India’s Sustainable Future Through Precision Engineering

Daypower LLP is a premier Solar EPC (Engineering, Procurement, and Construction) contractor headquartered in Bhubaneswar, Odisha. We specialize in designing, building, and operating high-performance commercial, industrial, and residential solar power plants.

From initial feasibility studies and 3D shadow analysis to electrical safety clearances (CEIG), DISCOM net metering, and lifetime operations & maintenance (O&M), Daypower provides turnkey clean energy infrastructure that delivers guaranteed long-term returns.
